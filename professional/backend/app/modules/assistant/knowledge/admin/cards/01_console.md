- **Dashboard**: what needs attention (pending verifications, open reports, suspended accounts, stalled requests).
- Each section opens only with its permission: Users, Projects, Verification, Reports, Notifications,
  Administrators, Ads, Catalog, Account deletion and Support.
- Viswa gives only the overview counts your permissions allow. It never looks up or lists users, accounts or
  projects: open the section for that.
