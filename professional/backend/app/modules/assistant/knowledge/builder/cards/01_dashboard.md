Menu: **Dashboard**, **Projects**, **Portfolio**, **Requests**, **My Partners** (your site engineers),
**Find Clients**, **Buy & Sell** and **Product Catalog**. The top bar has messages, the notification bell and
your avatar menu (Profile Settings, Help & Feedback, Logout).
- **Dashboard**: new requests from house owners, with Accept / Reject, and your figures.
- **Projects**: Current, Previous and Upcoming projects. Open one for Site Activity, Project Analysis,
  Live Camera, Team, Overview and Payments.
