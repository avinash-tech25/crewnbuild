- House owners near you send you requests for their projects. They appear on the **Dashboard** and in **Requests**.
- **Accept** a request with your quotation amount, or **Reject** it. The owner compares quotations and may
  negotiate an offer with you.
- When the owner chooses you and you confirm, the project is awarded to you and appears in **Projects**.
- **Requests** also lists the requests you sent.
