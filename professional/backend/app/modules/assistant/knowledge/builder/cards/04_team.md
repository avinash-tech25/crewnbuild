- **My Partners** is your site engineer team: invite an engineer by e-mail, or accept an engineer's join request.
- Assign a site engineer to a project from the project's **Team** tab.
- Suppliers you work with are accepted through requests; assign a supplier to a project from the project's
  **Team** tab so the project's site engineer can order from them.
