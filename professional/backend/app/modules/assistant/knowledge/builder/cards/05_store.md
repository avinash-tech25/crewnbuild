- Each project has a materials store kept by its site engineer: stock goes up when a supplier delivers an order
  and down when the site engineer records usage. Ask Viswa what is in stock on a project.
- Daily site records (labour, materials, expenses) are written by the site engineer; open the project's
  **Site Activity** and **Project Analysis** for the records and the cost totals.
