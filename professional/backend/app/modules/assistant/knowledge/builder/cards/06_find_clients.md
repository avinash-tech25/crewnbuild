- **Find Clients** lists open projects of house owners near your pincode; send them a request.
- **Portfolio** shows your completed work to owners. Keep your profile complete in **Profile Settings** so
  owners trust it.
- **Buy & Sell** is for property; **Product Catalog** lists construction products from suppliers.
