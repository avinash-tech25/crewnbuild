Sign in:
- Enter your email (your user name also works) and password, then tap **Sign in**.
- Or use **Google** / **Microsoft** under "or continue with". If your email already has a password account, sign in with the password first.
- "Invalid username/email or password" means the email or password is wrong. Try Forgot password.
- "This account has been suspended" means you need to contact support.

Forgot password:
1. Open Forgot password, enter your email, tap **Send reset code**.
2. Enter the 6-digit code from your email, tap **Verify OTP**.
3. Enter and confirm the new password, tap **Update password**, then sign in.
The same rules as sign up apply (10 minutes, 30 seconds before a new code, 5 tries). This also lets a Google-only account set a password.

Sign out:
- Avatar menu (top right) -> **Logout**.
- Note: clicking the logo or opening the sign-in page also signs you out. If you got logged out suddenly, that is why; just sign in again.
- A session that expires signs you out automatically.
