Where: avatar menu (top right) -> **Profile Settings** -> **Edit**.

Fields: Name, **Phone**, **Pincode**, Address, **Country of Residence** (India), profile image (**Add Image**). Tap **Save**.
For a house owner the required ones are phone, address and pincode.

- Phone: exactly **10 digits**, without +91 (e.g. 9876543210).
- Pincode: exactly **6 digits**. On Complete Profile it is checked with India Post and you choose your post office branch. In Profile Settings the pincode fills the area in, and the address too if it is empty.

When it is needed: you can browse without a complete profile, but sending or accepting requests and buying materials need it. The app takes you to the profile form and brings you back after.

Errors:
- "This phone number is already in use." means another account has it.
- "Enter a valid 10 digit phone number." or "Enter a valid 6 digit pincode." means check the digits.

Note: saving your profile with an address when you have no project yet may create a project named "General". You can edit or delete it from Projects.
