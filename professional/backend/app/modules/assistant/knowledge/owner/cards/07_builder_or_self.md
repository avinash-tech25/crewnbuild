You choose this when you create a project, and it **can't be changed later**.

**Hire Builder** (most people):
- A builder runs the whole job: workers, materials and the site engineer.
- You find builders, compare quotations, negotiate, and assign one.
- Best if you don't have time or construction experience.

**Self Construction**:
- No builder. You hire a **site engineer** yourself and buy materials from **suppliers** in the app.
- More control over each material and cost, but you manage more yourself.
- Needed for "Order Product" and buying materials in the app.

If unsure: suggest Hire Builder for first-time owners. The final choice depends on their time, experience and budget, so this is general advice.
