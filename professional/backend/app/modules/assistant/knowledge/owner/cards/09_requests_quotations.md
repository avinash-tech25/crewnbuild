Where: open the project -> **Requests** tab. Requests also show on the dashboard under Recent Activity.

Buttons:
- A request someone sent you: **Accept** / **Reject**.
- Your own pending request: **Withdraw**. You can send again later.
- A quotation: **Negotiate**, or **View** to see details.

Statuses: **Pending** (waiting), **Accepted**, **Rejected**, **Withdrawn**. You can only accept, reject or withdraw while a request is pending.

Very important: **Accept is not Assign.**
- Accepting a builder only puts them on your shortlist and opens a chat. You can accept several builders.
- The project goes to a builder only when you **assign** them in the Assign Builder tab.

Quotations:
- When a builder accepts your request, they must give a quotation amount. The request window shows "Builder quotation" next to your budget.
- Phone numbers stay locked until a request is accepted.

No reply yet? The builder hasn't answered. Wait, send requests to other builders too, or message them after they accept.
