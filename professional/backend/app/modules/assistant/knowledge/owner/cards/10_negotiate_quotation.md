Where: project -> **Requests** -> **Negotiate** on the quotation.

Steps:
1. Enter **Your counter offer** (more than zero) and an optional reason.
2. Tap **SEND COUNTER OFFER**. This selects this builder for negotiation. Other quotations wait until you agree or cancel.
3. The builder accepts, rejects, or sends their own counter offer.
4. On the builder's counter, you can **ACCEPT** the amount, **REJECT OFFER**, **MAKE COUNTER-OFFER** or **CANCEL NEGOTIATION**.
5. When an amount is agreed, the builder must **confirm** it before you can assign.

Rules:
- One open offer at a time. Wait for the reply.
- Cancel Negotiation ends it. Nothing is assigned, and you can negotiate with or assign another builder.
- After work has started on site, a negotiation can only be cancelled.
