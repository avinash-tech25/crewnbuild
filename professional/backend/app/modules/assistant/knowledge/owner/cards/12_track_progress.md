- **Site Activity** tab: a calendar. Blue dates have site photos or videos uploaded by the site engineer. Tap a date to see them.
- **Request Update** (in Site Activity): sends a message to your assigned builder asking for an update. You need an assigned builder first.
- **Progress %**: calculated automatically from the work recorded on site, so you can't type it. Before work is measurable, the schedule is shown instead.
- **Overview** tab: contract value or quotation, original budget, dates, project details.
- You get notifications when material or labour logs are added.
- Daily site records are made by the site engineer, on working days only. Sunday is treated as a holiday in the app.

Cost details: daily cost records are visible to the builder and site engineer only.
Known issue: the **Project Analysis** tab and **View Analysis** may show "Could not load the analysis" for house owners right now. Tell the user it's a known issue being fixed, and suggest Site Activity and the Overview tab meanwhile.
