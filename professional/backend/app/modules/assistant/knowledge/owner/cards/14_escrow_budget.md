- Every project gets three **escrow milestones**: **Foundation 25%**, **Structure 45%** and **Finishing 30%** of the budget. After you assign a builder, they are recalculated on the contract value.
- See them in the project's **Escrow** tab (and the **Payments** tab).

Important, be honest with the user:
- These milestones are a **payment plan shown for planning only**. Right now **no money is paid, held or released through Crew&Build**. They always show "pending".
- Pay your builder directly as agreed in your contract.
- Online payment for milestones is not available in the app yet.

Budget:
- You set it when creating the project, or it comes from an applied estimate. Change it in **Overview -> Edit** ("Original budget").
- Changing the budget later does not resize the milestones.
- The project shows **Contract value** once a builder is confirmed and assigned.

Money disputes or a builder not delivering: treat this as escalate (support). Never promise refunds.
