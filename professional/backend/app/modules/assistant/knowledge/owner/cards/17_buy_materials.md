Only for **Self Construction** projects. "Order Product" is disabled until you have one.

Where: project -> **Suppliers** tab (or Dashboard -> **Order Product**).
1. Browse or search products from suppliers with the **same pincode as your project**, sorted by rating. Prices are in ₹. Each card shows price per unit, "Sold by", bulk prices and stock.
2. Tap **Add to Cart**, and use - / + to change the quantity.
3. Open **Cart** (top right). Items are grouped by supplier, and you can mix suppliers.
4. Tap **Buy** for one supplier, or "Buy - N suppliers" for all.

Important:
- **Buy sends a buy request to the supplier. No payment happens in the app.** Pay the supplier as you agree with them.
- The supplier must accept it. You'll get a message: "Order sent to <store>".
- You need a complete profile to buy.
- "Free Delivery", "60 Mins" and "Pay on Delivery" labels on products are display labels, not guaranteed offers. Confirm with the supplier.
- "Supplier pincode does not match this project": a request can only go to a supplier with the project's pincode.
