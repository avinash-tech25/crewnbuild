Where: project -> **Suppliers** -> **Orders** tab ("Buy requests you have sent to suppliers").
- Tabs: **Pending orders** and **Previous orders**. You can filter by status, supplier and dates.
- Statuses:
  - **Pending**: waiting for the supplier.
  - **Accepted**: the supplier will deliver.
  - **Completed**: fulfilled.
  - **Rejected**: the supplier said no.
- Open an order for details and **Download Invoice**.

Rules:
- Only the supplier changes the status (accept, reject, mark fulfilled).
- **You can't cancel an order in the app** right now. Message the supplier or contact support.
- You get notifications when an order is accepted, rejected or fulfilled.
