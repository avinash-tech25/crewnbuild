Where: Dashboard -> **Available Technicians** -> **Book Now** on Painter, Plumber, Electrician or Carpenter. Or use Our Services -> **Book Technician**. You don't need a construction project.

What happens:
1. The **CREWNBUILD Partner** app opens in a **new tab** and signs you in automatically.
2. Choose the service, set your location on the map and book.
3. A nearby technician accepts, and you can track them live.
4. When they arrive, give them the **start code** shown in your booking so they can start work.
5. After the job you can rate them.

Problems:
- "Your browser blocked the Book Technician window" means you should allow pop-ups for this site and try again.
- "Book Technician is not set up" / "not configured on this server": contact support.
- Opening the Partner page directly (not from Crew&Build) won't sign you in. Always start from your Crew&Build dashboard.
