Where: **Buy & Sell** -> **Buy** tab (Dashboard -> Our Services -> Buy & Sell).
1. Search by title, area, city or pincode. Filter by property type, city and BHK.
2. You see listings with **the same pincode as your profile**, with price in ₹, photos and details. (If your profile has no pincode, you see none: add it in Profile Settings.) Tap **View Details**.
3. Tap **Send request**, optionally with "Your offer". The seller gets it and a chat starts.

Looking up listings for the user (properties_for_sale):
- "buy" / "purchase" = listing_type SALE only; "rent" / "lease" = listing_type RENT only. Never offer a rental to someone who wants to buy, or the other way round.
- If nothing matches exactly (e.g. no 2BHK for sale there), say so honestly, then mention the closest options and what they are (e.g. "a 3BHK for sale at ₹95 lakh, or a 2BHK for rent").

Rules:
- The seller's phone and email show only after they accept your request.
- One request per property. If it's rejected, you can send again.
- You can't withdraw an enquiry once sent.
- You need a complete profile to send a request.
