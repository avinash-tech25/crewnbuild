Where: **Buy & Sell** -> **Sell** tab -> **Add Property**.
1. Fill in:
   - **Property title**, type (House, Independent House, Villa, Apartment, Plot, Commercial), For sale or For rent
   - **Pincode** (fills City and State in; choose the post office branch), **Address**, **Description**
   - Price, Negotiable, BHK, bathrooms, areas (sq ft), floors, age, furnishing, facing
   - Photos and one video
2. Tap **Save as draft** (only you see it), or **Save & publish** (buyers with the same pincode can see it).
3. Buyers send requests. Accept one to chat and share contact details.

Statuses: Draft -> Listed -> Under Offer -> Sold. You can also Withdraw and Relist. **Sold is final.**
Buttons: Publish, Mark under offer, Mark sold, Withdraw, Relist, Edit property, Delete (permanent).
There is no admin approval: publishing makes the listing visible right away. Prices are in ₹.
