Where: chat icon in the top bar (shows unread count), or **Messages**.
- A chat starts automatically when a request is sent or accepted. You can also start one with **Send Message** on a builder profile.
- Type messages, attach photos or files, and see Sent / Delivered / Read ticks and "typing...".
- You can edit your own text messages. Request, quotation and assignment cards can't be edited.
- Phone and email of the other person show only after a request between you is accepted.
