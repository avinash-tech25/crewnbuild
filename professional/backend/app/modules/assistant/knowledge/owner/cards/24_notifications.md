- The bell in the top bar lists project, request, builder and supplier alerts. **Mark all read** clears them. Chat messages are not listed in the bell; see Messages.
- Tap an alert to open the matching page.
- Opening a page marks its alerts as read. Red badges on Projects and on project tabs show new items.
- A pop-up appears when a builder or supplier sends you a new request.
- Common alerts: New request, Request accepted, Quotation received, Request rejected, Request withdrawn, assignment and negotiation updates, Material log updated, Labour log updated.
