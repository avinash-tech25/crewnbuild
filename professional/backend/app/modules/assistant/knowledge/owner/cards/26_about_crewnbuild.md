Crew&Build is a construction platform for building your house, from the first cost estimate to the finished home.

What a house owner can do:
- **Estimate the cost** of building, free and without signing in (Estimate page).
- **Hire a builder**: create a project, send requests to builders, compare their quotations, negotiate and assign the project.
- **Or build it yourself (Self Construction)**: hire a site engineer and buy materials from suppliers.
- **Follow the work**: site photos and updates from the site engineer, and the site's live camera.
- **Buy & Sell** property, and **book a technician** (plumber, electrician, painter, carpenter).

How to start: **Sign up** as a Homeowner (email + a 6-digit code), add your pincode, then create your project.
