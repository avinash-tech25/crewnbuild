Builders, suppliers and site engineers join on the same **Sign up** page: under "I am joining as a", choose your type instead of Homeowner.

How it helps them:
- **Builders**: house owners with the same pincode find you in **Find Builders** and send requests; you reply with a quotation, can negotiate, and get the project when the owner assigns it. Builders can join free or choose a paid plan at sign up.
- **Suppliers**: list your materials with prices; house owners building themselves (Self Construction) add them to their cart and send you buy requests.
- **Site engineers**: house owners building themselves send you requests; once assigned, you record the daily site work and photos.

After sign up you add your pincode (house owners are matched with businesses in the same pincode) and complete your business profile.
