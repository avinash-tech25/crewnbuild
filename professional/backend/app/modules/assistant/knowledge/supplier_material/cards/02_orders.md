- A buyer's **Buy** arrives in **Requests** (purchase requests): **Accept** or **Reject** the whole request.
- **Orders** lists every order line: accept, reject or **mark fulfilled** when delivered. Fulfilling lowers
  your stock and adds the material to the buyer's project store.
